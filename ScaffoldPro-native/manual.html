ScaffoldPro User Manual

    How to use every page, button and workflow in ScaffoldPro, from a new client to a paid invoice. Each screenshot has numbered markers: rest the pointer on a number, or on its line beside the picture, to see which control it means. Click a picture to enlarge it.

    For ScaffoldPro as of Batch 149 · October 2026

    How ScaffoldPro is organised

    ScaffoldPro keeps all of the company's work in one place: clients and sites, projects, and every document a project needs (BOQs, quotations, delivery schedules, delivery notes, invoices and letters), plus stock, accounting, marketing, staff and the team's tasks and chat.

    The sidebar

    The sidebar on the left is always there. Its tabs are grouped under four headings:

    Overview

    Dashboard (your day at a glance), Calendar (everything with a date) and Tasks (the team's to-dos).

    Team

    Chat (messages, with an unread count) and Team (people, director signatures and announcements).

    Operations

    Costs (material lists and manpower rates), Clients & Sites, Projects and Stock.

    Company

    Accounting, Marketing, Admin (workers, company documents, employees and payroll), Settings, and this User Manual.

    At the top of the sidebar is Search (⌘K). At the bottom are who the data is shared with and your own User page (⌘0).

    Documents and how they're linked

    Every document belongs to a project, and documents are made from one another. Each kind has its own muted colour (a thin stripe or dot), so it can be spotted anywhere in the app:

    The document chain

    Linked documents share a number: the quotation made from BQ26212-001 is Qt26212-001, and the delivery notes and invoices made from it are DN26212-001 and H26212-001 (a second one is H26212-001-2). Letters (lavender) sit beside the chain, and drawings and documents (grey) can be filed with a BOQ or quotation.

    Status: Draft, Issued, Cancelled

    Draft→Issued→CancelledInvoices also:Partially PaidPaidOverdue
    • Draft: everything can be changed. Its PDF has a large grey DRAFT watermark across every page.
    • Issued: sent to the client. The document is locked; set it back to Draft to change it. An issued document asks you to type its number before it can be deleted.
    • Cancelled: kept for the records, marked CANCELLED, and can't be reopened. An issued invoice with a mistake is cancelled, never deleted.

    Everyday controls

    These work the same way on every page.

    Search ⌘KFinds projects, clients, sites, BOQs, quotations, invoices, delivery notes, letters, workers, drawings, documents and material-list items. Use ↑ ↓ to move and Return to open.
    Undo / Redo ⌘Z · ⇧⌘Z or ⌘YUndoes the last action: adding or removing a line, changing a quantity or price, Multiply, linking, a status change, a deletion, saving a client and more (up to 60 steps while the app is open). The page redraws in place, and what changed glows for a moment. A note at the bottom right says what was undone, with Redo. Exports, backups, chat and files on disk aren't undone.
    Back / Forward ⌘[ ⌘]The ‹ Back link at the top of a project or document returns to the page you came from.
    DatesType day first, dd/mm/yyyy (24/09/2026). 24/9/26, 24 Sep, 24092026 and "today" work too. Away from the box, a date shows as 24 Sep 2026. ↓ or a click opens the calendar, and Esc puts back what was there.
    Sums in boxesA quantity or unit-price box works out sums: 14+28, 2 x 7, (3+2)*4, 120/4. "= 42" shows as you type, the answer is saved, and a small corner mark shows that the box holds a sum. Click into the box to see the sum again.
    Moving between boxesIn a table, ↑ ↓ go to the box above or below, and ← → to the box beside it, like a spreadsheet. Tab or Return in a quantity saves it and moves to the next line's quantity. The arrows never change a number; rest the pointer on a number box for its − / + stepper.
    Lists and menusDrop-down lists, the Export button, ☰ menus and the page / language icons open when you rest the pointer on them (or click). A tick marks the current choice. Use ↑ ↓ and Return, or type to jump to a choice.
    Icon buttonsCommon actions show only an icon; rest the pointer on one for its name. They include Locate File (folder with magnifier), Delete (red bin), Print, Open, Edit, Duplicate, Show in Finder, Archive and Rename.
    DialogsScaffoldPro asks with its own dialogs. Delete-type questions have a red button named after the action. Return picks the highlighted button and Esc cancels.
    Drag and dropDrag files from Finder onto a project's Drawings or Documents, or onto a BOQ's or quotation's Drawings panel. Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to reorder lines, sections, pinned items and material-list items. The page scrolls when you drag near its edge.
    Sorting listsClick a column title to sort: the first click sorts ascending, the second descending, and the third puts the table back in its own order.
    PreviewsEvery PDF and Word export opens in a preview first (zoom with ⌘− / ⌘0 / ⌘+). Nothing is saved until Save to Project Folder (⌘S). After saving you can Open it, Show in Finder, or choose Done.
    Changes save by themselves as you type or leave a box, everywhere except Settings, which has a Save Settings bar. If you try to leave Settings with unsaved changes, it asks whether to save them.
    Everything respects System Settings › Accessibility › Reduce motion, and the app follows Light or Dark mode (Settings › General › Appearance).

    Dashboard

    Your own day at a glance: what's waiting on you, what you worked on, and what the team is doing. Open it with ⌘1.

    The panels

    Announcement barMessages for you, important ones first. × closes one on every Mac you use. A request to sign a quotation shows here with Review.
    My TasksYour open tasks. Tick one to finish it. The first row, New Task, adds one; All tasks opens Tasks.
    My Active & Recent ProjectsProjects you made, changed or worked on documents in, most recent first.
    My Quotations Awaiting ReplyIssued quotations with no signed copy yet, showing the monthly and one-time charges. The upload icon files the client's signed copy (or drop the PDF or photo onto the row); × marks it not needed (accepted by email, or not going ahead).
    Unpaid InvoicesCompany-wide, with what's overdue.
    My Recently Changed DocumentsGrouped by kind, with the most recent group on top.
    My Recent Delivery Notes · Scaffold Inspections DueYour latest notes, and inspections due or overdue on active projects.
    My Recent Activity · Recent Team ActivityYour own history entries, and everyone else's with their names.
    Needs Attention · Backup reminderWorker and company documents expiring within 30 days, and a reminder when there's been no backup for a week.

    Each list shows its first 5; Show 3 more adds more in place and Show fewer folds it back.

    Arranging the Dashboard

    1. Press Customise. A Widgets tray opens.
    2. Drag a panel by its bar to move it. A blue bar shows where it will go.
    3. Drag a panel's right edge to make it ¼, ½, ¾ or full width.
    4. Drag a panel into the tray (or press its ×) to remove it, and drag one out of the tray to add it.
    5. Reset Layout goes back to the standard layout. The layout is kept for you on this Mac.

    Calendar

    Everything with a date in one place: delivery days, delivery notes, inspections (done and due), tasks, quotation expiry dates, invoice due dates, lead follow-ups, expiring documents, and project start and finish dates.

    Keys: ← → move a day, ↑ ↓ a week, T today, W / M week or month, N new task, Page Up / Page Down a whole period.

    Rest the pointer on any item for a card with its details, and click it to open what it's about. In the month view, double-click a day (or use its +) to add a task, and +2 more shows the rest of that day in the side panel.

    Tasks

    The team's to-dos. A task can be for a project and for a person, with a due date, an optional time and High priority. Each project also has its own Tasks tab.

    Press N anywhere on the page to start a task, and / to search.

    Chat

    Messages with everyone, your team, or one person. The sidebar shows how many are unread. Between office Macs a message arrives in about a second.

    Team: people, signatures, announcements

    Who uses ScaffoldPro and on which devices, directors signing and chopping quotations, and announcements.

    On your own card, add your signature and the company chop as pictures (Choose Signature…, Choose Chop…). Directors need both to sign.

    Signing and chopping a quotation

    Signature workflow

    Only the director asked can sign, from their own Mac, with their own signature; the chop is placed over the signature. The quotation then shows "Signed & chopped by … on …" with Open Signed PDF, which shows the saved copy in the viewer with Done or a red Withdraw Sign & Chop (the quotation is no longer marked signed and the signed PDF goes to the Trash).

    Costs: materials and manpower

    What things cost and what we charge. Materials holds the SP and SCAFOM material lists, with each item's weight, monthly rental and sale price. Manpower Rates holds each kind of worker's rate and what each rate provider charges us. Open it with ⌘2.

    Unit Rates sheet for a client

    1. ☰ › Rates › Export rates › PDF switches the list into picking mode.
    2. Tick items, from either list (switching lists keeps the ticks), or use Select All Shown.
    3. Make Unit Rates PDF…: choose the client (their default markup is filled in), the mark up / down %, and an optional Re: line and remarks.
    4. The PDF (letterhead, "UNIT RATES": No., Item Description, Unit Weight in kg, Unit Monthly Rental, Unit Sale Price, both prices with the markup) opens in the preview. Save it to the Unit Rates folder.

    The providers set up are Summit Engineering & Resources Limited and Lingma Const. & Eng. Co. Ltd. Add or remove workers at the bottom of the table. Everything saves as you type.

    The material list is kept the same on every Mac, including pins, order and Chinese names, even on a Mac that isn't sharing a folder.

    Clients & Sites

    Clients on the left and sites on the right. A curved line joins a client to each site it has had a project at, and the line is thicker for more projects. Open it with ⌘3.

    A client has a company name, a reference (e.g. LM), a contact person, phone, email, Address Lines 1–3, city, postcode, country, a Default Markup % (new BOQs and quotations for them start with it), billing info and notes. Its details show its projects and documents. Archive hides a client without deleting anything.

    Drag a client's dot onto a site to start a new project for the two. The New Project form opens with both already chosen.

    Projects

    Every project, grouped by who created it and then by client. Open it with ⌘4.

    Overview: every project's documents, linked

    A bright green line means the two documents are linked (kept in step). A grey dashed line means a quotation was made from a BOQ but has since been unlinked. A violet branch joins a quotation to its subsidiaries. Rest the pointer on a document to light up its whole chain, and click any tile to open it.

    Making a project

    1. New Project. The number is proposed (e.g. 26214); tick Override to type your own (YYNNN).
    2. Name it, then choose the Client and Site. + Add New Client… and + Add New Site… at the top of the lists add one on the spot.
    3. Optional details: start date, expected completion, project manager, description and internal notes. Tick "Upload a drawing after creating the project" to go straight to the upload.
    4. Create Project. It gets its own folder in Documents › ScaffoldPro › Projects.

    Deleting a project

    Right-click it on Projects, or use Edit Details › Delete Project… on its page. An empty project goes after a simple confirmation. A project with anything in it lists what will go, and you must type its name or number first. Its documents and records are deleted and its folder goes to the Trash, so files can still be put back from there. Tasks and expenses are kept, without the project.

    Click the project number to copy it. The header also shows who made the project and who last worked on it.

    BOQ editor

    A bill of quantities lists the materials for a scaffold, with weights and rates. It prints as the company's landscape BQ sheet (orange "PROFICIENCY QUOTATION" banner, with prices) or on the portrait letterhead (no prices, total weight).

    Multiply quantities (sets)

    Line Items ☰ › Multiply quantities… scales quantities in one go: ×2 for two sets of the same scaffold, +10% spare, ½, or any number. Choose all items or tick lines and sections. Quantities stay whole numbers (round up, nearest or down). A preview shows each quantity before and after, and Undo puts the old ones back. On quotations and invoices only materials are scaled.

    A BOQ that's too long for one page is shrunk to fit (down to 70%), as Google Sheets' "Fit to page" does. Longer ones run on over several pages, with the banner and headings repeated.

    Quotation editor

    The priced offer to the client, on the letterhead (portrait) or as the landscape BQ sheet. This is the page used most, so it's covered in detail.

    The extra sections

    Delivery ChargesA dialog weighs the materials, picks the matching band (Under 500 kg $1,200 · 500 kg–1 t $1,800 · 1–2 t $2,200 · 2–6 t $3,300 · 6–8 t $3,800; editable in Settings), marks it Suggested, and over 8 tons suggests a split. Set trucks and trips (2 = delivery and collection). The lines go in their own bracket, D1, D2…, with + Add Delivery.
    Priced SectionsA title (e.g. Design Fees, Mandatory Inspection Fees) and rows A1, A2… with No. · Description · Qty · Unit Price · Per · Total, charged once / per day / per week / per month. Added to the total and not marked up.
    Standard Manpower Rates"Erection & Dismantle Manpower Rates": R1 Scaffolder CP, R2 Scaffolder, R3 Rigger, R4 General Helper, each "(Rate Only)" with the overtime note. The rates come from Costs › Manpower Rates. Fill Standard Rates adds any that are missing.
    NotesA note across the table, in small grey italics, after the total.

    Sections can be dragged into order (⋮⋮), renamed, and removed.

    A quotation linked to a BOQ

    Linking

    Click an item's green link mark to unlink just that item; it then keeps its own figures and shows "Unlinked · Relink…". Relink shows both sets of figures and asks which is right: the BOQ's, or this quotation's. Only drafts change; a document set back to Draft catches up.

    Splitting a quotation into subsidiaries

    Split and revert

    A subsidiary stays linked to the same BOQ. Documents made from it carry the same part (H26212-007-s1). When the main quotation is exported or printed, you're asked: Qt… Only, Choose… which subsidiaries, or Attach All. Attached ones follow the quotation's own pages.

    Delivery schedule

    Under every quotation and BOQ: which materials go to site on which day.

    Once a day has items on it, the schedule is printed automatically after the quotation's or BOQ's own pages, on one page when it fits (A4 landscape or portrait, else A3). The internal notes are never printed there. Delivery days show on the Calendar.

    Delivery notes

    What goes to site, by hand, with weights and no prices, laid out like the company's own (DN26038a).

    Issuing a delivery note books its items out of the yard (Stock): on hire to the project, or sold for a Sale quotation. Setting it back to Draft, or deleting it, books them in again.

    The signed copy, and its invoice

    Once a note is issued, a bar under its toolbar asks for the copy signed on site: Upload Signed Copy…, or drop the PDF or photo onto the bar. It's kept as "DN… - Signed.pdf" in the project's Delivery Notes folder, the note shows a green Signed tag in the project's list, and it's added after the pages of the invoice that bills it whenever that invoice is exported, printed or combined. Open, Locate File, Replace… and Remove are on the bar.

    Signed delivery notes

    On paper: Our Ref. / Site Ref. / Date, BY HAND ONLY, the details, the table (No | Item Description | Unit Weight | Qty | Total Weight) and the receipt lines (Received By, Date, Full Name, Contact No.). A note that only just spills onto a second page is squeezed onto one.

    Invoices and payments

    What's billed, and what's been paid.

    An invoice made from delivery notes lists them at the top under Signed delivery notes: each one's signed copy (Open), or Upload Signed Copy… for one not signed yet (or drop the file on its row). Every signed copy is added after the invoice's own pages when it's exported, printed or combined.

    Recording a payment

    1. Issue the invoice (payments can't be recorded on a draft or a cancelled one).
    2. Under the totals: the amount, the date, the method (bank transfer, cheque, cash…) and a reference, then Record Payment.
    3. The status follows by itself: Partially Paid while a balance remains, Paid at zero, Overdue after the due date. Overpayments are refused.

    Payments feed Accounting. Deleting an invoice removes its payments there too.

    Letters

    Letters on the letterhead, written like a Google Doc, with numbered annexures.

    Each project also has a Letters tab. Letters are numbered from Settings (e.g. L26001-001), and a letter written from Marketing › Promotions is numbered PL26-001.

    Attachments (annexures)

    1. Under the letter, + Add Attachment… picks PDFs or pictures (copies are kept with the letter).
    2. Each is numbered in order, ANNEXURE P.01, P.02…, with a line for what it is. Its files show as chips: click one to open it, × takes it off, + Files… adds more. ↑ / ↓ reorder the attachments.
    3. "Numbered" changes the prefix (e.g. "ANNEX ").
    4. The PDF lists them under "Attachments:" and adds, after the letter, a cover page for each (letterhead, recipient, annexure number and name) followed by its files.

    Sharing with other Macs, and ScaffoldPro Web

    Several people work on the same data, each on their own Mac, or in a browser on a PC, iPad or phone.

    How changes travel

    Each Mac writes only its own change log in the shared folder, so iCloud never has two copies of one file to choose between. The newest change to each record wins, and deletions carry across.

    Setting up sharing (Settings › Share with Other Macs)

    1. On the first Mac: Share My Data… makes a "ScaffoldPro Team" folder in the iCloud Drive folder you choose and copies everything in.
    2. Share that folder in Finder with the others.
    3. On each other Mac: Join a Shared Folder… (that Mac's own data is kept aside; Stop Sharing on This Mac… brings it back).
    4. If macOS asks to find devices on the local network, or to accept incoming connections, choose Allow.

    ScaffoldPro Web (for PCs, iPads and phones)

    1. On the office Mac: Settings › Web Access. Set an office password, then tick Turn on Web Access.
    2. On a PC, open one of the addresses shown (e.g. http://office-mac.local:8642) and sign in with your name and the password.
    3. Keep ScaffoldPro open on that Mac (it won't let the Mac sleep while Web Access is on; closing its window quits it).

    People in a browser appear on the Team page as themselves ("Safari on Mac via Harry's Mac mini (web)"). PDFs open as downloads. Choosing files to upload, imports, backups and setup need a Mac. From outside the office, use the free Tailscale app on the Mac and the device.

    Google Sheets overview

    A Google Sheet that shows who did what, and when, kept in step with ScaffoldPro both ways.

    Overview tab

    Who did how much in the last 7 days, projects by status, the latest 25 things done.

    Activity tab

    Everything done in ScaffoldPro, newest first. Type a new row (no ID) such as “Site visit — checked ties” and it's added to ScaffoldPro's history.

    Projects tab

    Every project. Change Status, Project Manager or Notes here and ScaffoldPro is updated, and the other way round.

    When it syncs

    About every minute while ScaffoldPro is open on the Mac it's set up on, and 20 seconds after a save. If both sides change between syncs, the sheet wins.

    Connecting it (Settings › Google Sheets)

    Once connected, Settings shows "In step — synced 1 min ago", any changes taken in from the sheet, or what went wrong, with Open Sheet, Sync Now and Disconnect….

    Backups, updates and installing

    Automatic backupsEvery day at 12:00 a.m. and 12:00 p.m. (or as soon as the Mac is awake and ScaffoldPro open). Kept for a week.
    Create BackupSettings › Backup & Restore (⇧⌘B): everything (database, project folders, administration documents) in one dated folder, kept until you delete it.
    RestorePick a backup from the list, or Restore from Folder…. A "before restore" safety backup is always made first. Restore is off while sharing a folder.
    Local Copy in DocumentsWhile sharing, Documents › ScaffoldPro is kept up to date as a local copy (Copy Now, Show in Finder).
    Automatic iCloud BackupA copy in iCloud Drive › Proficiency › William's Work, about a minute after saving and every 15 minutes, with the database kept for each of the last 30 days. Nothing is deleted from it.
    UpdatesChecked when ScaffoldPro opens and every 30 minutes. Update Now saves what's open, makes a backup, builds the new version and reopens. Later asks again in an hour. ScaffoldPro › Check for Updates… checks now.
    InstallingDouble-click Install ScaffoldPro (in the Scaffold-Pro folder). It sets up the tools, signs in to GitHub, gets the latest version, builds and opens it.
    Closing ScaffoldPro's window quits it. Anything being typed is saved first.

    Questions and fixes
